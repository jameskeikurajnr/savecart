Save Cart – Support

Save Cart helps you spend less every time you shop. Write your list with prices before you go, set a spend limit and a time limit, and watch both on a dial while you shop. Anything you add that was not on your list is marked, so impulse buys are easy to spot. When you finish, you get a receipt that shows what you kept.

Getting started

  1. Write your list. On the Start screen tap Add items, then type or paste your list, one item per line or separated by commas. Add a price after a name if you know it, for example Milk 2L $3.10.
  2. Pick your store, your spend limit and your time limit. The plan card at the top shows what your list is estimated to cost and how much of your limit stays in your pocket.
  3. Tap Start shopping. The clock starts. Put things in your cart as you go.
  4. Tap Finish when you are done. You see your receipt, and you can save the trip to History.

Common questions

How do I add a price to an item on my list?

Two ways. Type it after the name when you write the list (Bread $4.20 or Bread 4.20). Or open the List tab while shopping, tap the item and type the price, then tap Save estimate. A whole number on its own, like "Eggs 12", is treated as part of the name, so only $4 or 4.20 count as prices.

What does "planned" and "unplanned" mean?

An item is planned when it was on your list. If you add something with the + button that is not on your list, it is unplanned: it gets a pink UNPLANNED sticker, a pink banner appears above your cart, and you get one warning (with a vibration if that is on). If you type the name of something that is on your list, Save Cart counts it as planned. If you start a trip with no list, nothing is called unplanned.

You can turn the warning off in Settings → Warn about unplanned items.

What is "Later"?

If you are not sure about something, tap Save for later when you add it, or tap an item in your cart and choose Move to Later. Items in Later are not counted toward your limit. You can move them to your cart at any time.

What are Need and Want?

Mark each item as a Need or a Want. Tap the Need/Want tag on a cart item to switch it. The dial screen and your receipt show how much of the trip was wants.

What does the dial show?

The dial shows how much of your spend limit is used, how much is left, and how much time is left. It turns red when you go over. "Time cost" is what your time in the store has been worth at the hourly amount you set on the Start screen (Your time is worth).

How do the warnings work?

In Settings you can choose when you are warned about your budget (a percentage of your limit) and when you are warned about time (minutes left). Each warning shows once per trip. The phone can also vibrate for warnings (Vibrate on warnings).

What is the stamp card?

On the receipt screen you will see a card with 10 circles. Each saved trip that stays within your limit earns a stamp. If you save a trip that goes over, the card starts again. A trip with nothing in the cart does not earn a stamp. Trips you do not save never change your card. Fill all 10 and you have completed a card.

What is saved in History?

When you tap Save to History on the receipt, the trip's numbers (store, date, amounts, time, and the planned/unplanned split) are saved on your phone. You can see your trips from the History button on the Start screen and clear them in Settings.

Where is my data? Is it sent anywhere?

Everything stays on your device. Save Cart has no account, no servers and no tracking, and it works without the internet. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Clear your trips in Settings → History, clear your list with Clear list, or delete the app from your phone, which removes everything it stored.

Can I change the look?

Yes. In Settings choose a theme: Tide, Gauge, Coin ring or Daylight. Each one changes the dial and the colours.

My keyboard covers a box

Tap the black Done button just above the keyboard to put it away, or scroll the page. If a box is still hidden, please email me with your phone model and a screenshot.
