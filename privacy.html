Save Cart – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: Save Cart does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal information. Everything you put into the app stays on your own device.

Who we are

Save Cart is made by James Jnr Keikura, an independent developer in Sydney, Australia. In this policy, "we", "us" and "the developer" mean James Jnr Keikura, and "the app" means Save Cart.

What the app collects

Nothing. Save Cart has:

  • no account, sign-up or login
  • no advertising and no ad tracking
  • no analytics, usage tracking or third-party code that collects data
  • no servers of our own. The app works offline and never sends your information to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your own device only:

WhatWhy
Your shopping list, with any prices you typeSo your list is there next time you open the app
The trip in progress (items, amounts, start time)So closing the app mid-shop loses nothing
Your saved trips in History (store, date, amounts, time, planned/unplanned split)So you can look back at your trips
Your stamp cardTo count trips in a row under your limit
Your settings (store, limits, hourly amount, theme, warnings)So the app opens the way you left it

All of this is worked out and stored on your device. It is not uploaded, shared or backed up by us.

Permissions the app asks for

Save Cart does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendar, health data or anything else. Vibration warnings use the phone's vibration motor, which does not need a permission; you can turn them off in Settings.

The App Store, Google Play and Microsoft Store

When you download Save Cart, Apple, Google or Microsoft handles the download under their own privacy policies. They may give developers anonymous, combined statistics, such as the number of downloads or crash reports, but only if you have chosen to share them in your device's settings. This information does not identify you. See Apple's, Google's and Microsoft's privacy policies.

If you join a TestFlight beta, Apple may share the email address you used for the invitation, and any feedback or crash reports you choose to send, with the developer. These are used only to improve the app.

Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems. We don't add you to any mailing list or share your email with anyone, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

Save Cart is suitable for all ages. It does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because your information never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and encryption. We recommend keeping your device's software up to date.

Your choices

  • Clear your trips: Settings → History → clear.
  • Clear your list: the Clear list button on the List tab.
  • Delete everything: delete the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. If we ever start collecting any information, or ask for a new permission such as your location, we will say so here first, and the app will ask for your permission.
